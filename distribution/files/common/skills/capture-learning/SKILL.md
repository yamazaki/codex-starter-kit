# Capture learning

1. Identify the observed failure or repeated correction and the evidence that it is reusable.
2. Choose the narrowest durable destination: AGENTS rule, Skill instruction, reference, regression test, or decision record.
3. Preserve scope and exceptions; do not convert a one-off preference into a universal rule.
4. Avoid duplicating an existing source of truth.
5. Verify that the new guidance would have prevented the demonstrated failure without constraining unrelated work.
